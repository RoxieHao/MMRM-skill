# Expected Route

## Route

Route A: Randomized Efficacy MMRM.

## Rationale

- The study is randomized and placebo-controlled.
- The model includes treatment group, visit, and treatment-by-visit interaction.
- The key output is treatment group LSMean and Triferic group minus placebo group contrast.
- The analysis population is FAS for the direct MMRM TFL and FAS/PPS for MMRM-imputation secondary TFLs.
